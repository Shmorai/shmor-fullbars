The four UK networks

EE (BT Group), O2 (Virgin Media O2), Vodafone and Three own and run the UK’s mobile masts. Vodafone and Three merged in 2025 to form VodafoneThree and are combining their networks, while still selling under both brands for now. Every other mobile brand rents capacity on one of these networks.

Contract, SIM only or pay as you go?

TypeBest forWatch out for
Phone contractSpreading the cost of a new phoneTotal cost over 24–36 months; device plan and airtime may be split
SIM onlyKeeping your current phone12-month vs 30-day flexibility
Pay as you goLight or occasional usersCredit expiry and bundle rules

Buying the phone outright and pairing it with SIM only is often cheaper over two years. Work out both totals.

Check coverage where you actually are

Coverage claims are national; you care about your home, work and commute. Use each network’s postcode checker and the independent Ofcom checker. Ask neighbours which network works indoors. And remember: a brand gives you its host network’s coverage.

Your rights when you change

  • Text PAC to 65075 to get a code to keep your number, or STAC to 75075 to leave without it. Codes arrive free, by text, within a minute.
  • Your new provider must switch you within one working day of your request.
  • You can’t be charged for notice periods running after your switch date.
  • UK providers can no longer sell locked phones.

Last updated: 14 October 2026

Frequently asked questions

Which UK mobile network has the best coverage?
It depends on where you live and travel. Independent reports and Ofcom’s checker show coverage by area. Check your postcode rather than relying on national claims.
Is a cheaper brand worse than the network itself?
You get the same masts, but some brands have lower priority on busy masts, fewer extras (like roaming or wi-fi calling) or slower 5G. Check what the plan includes.
Do I need 5G?
Not necessarily. 5G can be faster where it’s strong, but good 4G is fine for most tasks. Many SIM-only and contract plans now include 5G at no extra cost.
