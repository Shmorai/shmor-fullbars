Mobile guide

Which network does my provider use?

Brands like giffgaff, Smarty, Mint Mobile and Visible don’t own masts. They’re MVNOs (mobile virtual network operators) renting space on a bigger network. Here’s who uses whom.

Europe

Most European countries have three or four network operators, with MVNOs on top. For example, Germany has Telekom, Vodafone, O2 Telefónica and newcomer 1&1; France has Orange, SFR, Bouygues Telecom and Free; Spain has Movistar, MasOrange, Vodafone and DIGI. See our Europe guide.

Why it matters

  • Coverage: an MVNO’s coverage is its host network’s coverage, so compare postcode maps for the host.
  • Price: MVNOs are often cheaper for the same masts.
  • Priority and extras: some MVNOs get lower priority on busy masts, slower 5G or fewer roaming options.
  • Changes: brands sometimes move networks. We check these regularly, but always confirm with the provider.

Last updated: 14 October 2026

Frequently asked questions

What is an MVNO?
A mobile virtual network operator: a brand that sells mobile plans using another company’s network instead of building its own masts.
Is giffgaff on O2?
Yes. giffgaff is owned by Virgin Media O2 and runs on the O2 network.
