One Touch Switch: how switching broadband works now

Switching broadband between different networks used to mean calling your old provider and juggling dates. With One Touch Switch, your new provider does it for you.

By the FullBars editorial team · 9 October 2026 · 1 min read

Photo: NordWood Themes on Unsplash

What changed

Ofcom required broadband and landline providers to support a “gaining provider-led” switching process across all fixed networks. It went live in September 2024, so you can now switch between, say, an Openreach-based provider, Virgin Media and a full fibre altnet without contacting your old provider first.

How it works

  1. Choose your new provider and order.
  2. Give your name, address and current provider (some may ask for an account number).
  3. Your new provider contacts the old one and sends you details of any exit fees or effects on other services.
  4. The switch happens on the agreed date; your old contract ends automatically.

Your protections

  • You shouldn’t pay notice period charges after your switch date.
  • You get clear information on any early exit fees before you commit.
  • Providers in Ofcom’s automatic compensation scheme pay out if a switch is delayed or an engineer misses an appointment.
